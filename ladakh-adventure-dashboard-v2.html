📍 Tourist Highlights

  • Shanti Stupa — Best sunset view over Leh.
  • Leh Palace — Historic royal palace.
  • Khardung La — One of the highest motorable passes.
  • Diskit Monastery — Giant Buddha statue.
  • Hunder Sand Dunes — Desert in the mountains.
  • Pangong Lake — Famous blue lake.
  • Hadimba Temple — Cedar forest temple.
